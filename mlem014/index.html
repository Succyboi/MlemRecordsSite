Mlem comes from a long lineage of ambitious dreamers, but life has not always been easy.

For this record Mlem reached out to some friends they made back at the shelter in ‘14. Mlem asked them to pick some tracks from their litter which otherwise might have been overlooked, just as Mlem had been before.

This record is Mlem’s way of showing us that we’re all purr-fect in our own ways. If we celebrate our differences we can let beauty grow in its own light.
