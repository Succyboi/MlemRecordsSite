Autogynephilic Lesbian Zero Forever is a story about avoidance, dysphoria, withdrawal and the desperate struggle to combat them.

There’s a lot of feelings I have towards this project, all of which are completely irrelevant. Though I’m sure, that if you sought for them, if you really tried, you would read my secrets. You’d feel the nerve endings I disregard, my neglect and mine thereof, would be yours. If anything, please enjoy ALZF as what it wears: Fun and chaotic dance music.

Autogynephilic Lesbian Zero Forever is a continuous mix from Amersfoort Tracks onwards. I personally recommend you listen to it as such, preferably right after waking up, on the tram.

Downloadable versions of the EP come with an additional track that could not be shipped with streaming versions.
