There are many worldly problems cishet people experience: Money corrupting all our tech and resources, the cost of living crisis, Discord age verification, etc.

However, we the gays at Mlem Records experience one foremost injustice: The tragic divide between ppl who read about guys kissing vs ppl who read about girls kissing. We attempted to bridge this divide the only way we know how: Slicing breaks, synthesizing stabs and sampling copyrighted material.

These junglists believed in eachother’s cratedigging skills so much that they laid down their arms and embraced one another on this very remix exchange.

The kind of music that substance indulgence, workaholism and homosexuality produce: Fast unapologetic break tunes by two gay ass DJs.
