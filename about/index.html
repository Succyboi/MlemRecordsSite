About

Mlem Records is a netlabel and artist collective run by aliens in cyberspace.

Netlabel?

Yeah!

Netlabels are record labels that distribute music online. To us, being a netlabel means we love making music and we love giving that music to friends. So we prioritise accessibility, independence and fun.

Any Mlem release is yours now! We’ve already given it to you, friend! Please share stuff with more friends if you think they might like it. Sharing is caring! ♥

Internally Mlem functions like a worker cooperative. The label is run as a collective and is directed by its members, in equal vote.

Talk

Got a question? Wanna talk to a person? Wanna release something?

Send a message to @mlemrecords on indieweb.social. Or send an electronic mail to hi@mlemrecords.com or support@mlemrecords.com.

Donate

Mlem is entirely financed by its artists. If you like the things Mlem does and want to support its activities, please listen to the music, come to the shows and/or donate. All proceeds go towards making more music and we appreciate every bit of your patronage.

Ps: Send your receipt to hi@mlemrecords.com and DJ ZITwerK will send you some cool audio files as a thank you. <3

Licensing

Most Mlem releases1 are licensed under CC BY-NC or CC BY-NC-SA.

In short, that means you can freely download, share and remix Mlem releases. But can’t use them in commercial works by default. Just ask, we’re nice! (●’◡’●)

Demos

We would love if you sent us audio files. Please send us publicly accessible links that we can stream from and a short message about you and your intentions.

Hit us up at demos@mlemrecords.com.
