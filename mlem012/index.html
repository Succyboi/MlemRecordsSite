Elektronische Meuk Vol. 1 is a collection of tracks, of music that I like to make. While at first these tracks don’t seem to have a red line connecting them, by being made by the same artist they inherently share the same sonic palate and feelings that I, the artist, put into them.

This album features electronic music with a focus on texture. Containing feelings ranging from confusion, melancholy, monachopsis, lagging behind everyone else, to restlessness, sudden boosts in energy that you don’t know what to do with and many more.

This album exists because I want to release things, for the sake of creation. To make music, be able to share it with people and thereby share a little piece of me.
