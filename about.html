MlemRecords

Mlem Records is a netlabel in cyberspace.

Netlabel?

Yeah!

Netlabels are record labels that distribute music online.
To us, being a netlabel means that we love making music and we love giving that music to friends.

Any Mlem release is yours now! We've already given it to you, friend! You just never knew.

Please share stuff with more friends if you think they might like it. Sharing is caring! ♥

Licensing

Mlem releases are licensed under CC BY-NC unless listed otherwise.

In short, that means you can freely download, share and remix Mlem releases. But can't use them in commercial works by default. Just ask, we're nice! (●'◡'●)
