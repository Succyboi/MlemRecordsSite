WTR (Runnin’)

by M2ND

→ Bandcamp
→ Archive
⤓ Download Thanks for downloading! If you liked this single please consider donating. That way M2ND can make more!

Runnin’ is an energetic, sonic social criticism with hints of desperation. Condemning the busyness of life and the policies made by our government regarding ‘culture fees’.

Inspired by local punk bands, dance, ska and noise.
