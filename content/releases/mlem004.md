---

Wacko_ follows a narrative of “weirdifaction” —a bright sonic world that gradually unveils a creeping sense of absurdity. The album is the first album written, and recorded, by Cage.log. It is an IndieTronica/Art-pop album that blends Jazz, Funk, IDM, and Ambient influences.
