---

17 pieces of peace written between the chaotic ages of 14-19.

One day my sibling called me up while I was pondering, lying on my brand new mattress after I’d just moved out. Saying: “Hey chef, remember those songs we recorded?” And of course I did, that’s what had been on my mind. That’s what I was lying there for.
