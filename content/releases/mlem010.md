---

A collection of exquisite, artisanal break-based hits in celebration of Mlem Records' one year anniversary!

These tracks are distilled, high-fructose breakbeat re-arranging liquid. On here Aching Hands strangles Jerma, DJ ZITwerK goes 1 bpm too high, ENNO murders the subwoofer and the label bankrupts over the ammo spent on the intro track's gunshots.

A tribute to this bizarre collective, our friendship and the beautiful music and adventures it's spawned.

Big up all massive <3.
